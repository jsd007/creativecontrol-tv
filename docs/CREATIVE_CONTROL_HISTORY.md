## Themes that keep returning

- Being present before the world is watching
- Informal access vs. industry credentials
- Chicago as source, New York as acceleration, the rest of the world as later geography
- Empathy as a camera practice (Chike on Coodie — Tudum)
- Authorship: the name *Creative Control* is a working ethic, not a logo lockup
- Sports and music as twin documentary subjects
- Physical tapes that outlived their original documentary plan

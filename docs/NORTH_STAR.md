# North Star

CreativeControl.tv is not a filmmaker portfolio and not a wrapper around YouTube or Netflix.

It is an evolving proof-of-concept for a cultural archive and media platform that may be shown to Coodie Simmons, Chike Ozah, and the Creative Control team.

The goal is **not complete**. This file answers the central question from the **live product** (`https://creativecontrol-tv.vercel.app`, 2026-09-09) — not a wish list. Surface inventory: `docs/CONCEPTS.md`. The walk: `docs/PRESENTATION.md`.

## Central question

**How should people experience an archive this important?**

## The answer (from the live house)

jeen-yuhs.com was one story encountered by moving around a television. After that, the archive itself should feel like the **place those stories were kept** — not another remote-control toy, not the Framer reel splash, not a second documentary site.

**One archive. Many lenses.** The same catalog. You do not get six products. You get six ways to stand in one vault, then a frame.

You do not drop into a grid. You enter a **threshold**. Four rooms around James’s official still (white knot over CREATIVE CONTROL — `public/brand/creative-control-mark.jpg`) — ARCHIVE / HISTORY / DOCUMENT / SPAN. The mark stays mounted. The room changes: house door, screening room, leftover / mandate, year horizon. Choose a door. The live `creativecontrol.tv` splash is still a different surface.

Then the house:

| You stand in | Route | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Index** | `/archive` | A finding aid. Year as a beat. Typed rows. Ask a sentence. Ye + Chicago + 2002 + Studio = **1 RECORD** (Basement · MEMBERS ONLY). Not a thumbnail mosaic. |
| **The World** | `/world` | Memory on a Chicago-weighted R3F globe (desktop). Authored coasts, night-side, gold limb. MAP is fallback only (narrow / reduced-motion / failed WebGL). Not surveyed GIS. Not globe.gl. |
| **Television** | `/tv` | Programmed **CC-TV**. CH 07 airs the **366** official public uploads (ident first, then blocks / year books, PLAY · MUTED). Channels **00–06 / 08** stay house cuts. Mute remainder after. No invented show names. |
| **The Tapes** | `/tapes` | A vault aisle of facing spines (STORAGE / DIGITAL VAULT / PHONE / STUDIO / BROADCAST / UNOPENED). A person held a camera. Broadcast years are holdings, not camera originals. |
| **The Timeline** | `/timeline` | 1994—2026 as through-lines (`?through=ye`), not ALL / COODIE / CHIKE tabs. The span is the story until a name is followed. Year cells are proving stills, not a poster wall. |
| **The Clip** | `/clip/[slug]` | A frame in the gate, pulled from a labeled cassette. Official embed when there is a `youtubeId`. Closed visibility is a sleeve, not a 404. |
| **Collections** | `/collections` | Editorial cuts of the same catalog. Classics is **19** house titles, not 366 official uploads. Studio Nights is **0** public frames. Not on the rail. |

**DISCOVER** offers a house tape first — random cassette, somewhere else, Unseen, on this day, from Chicago, Coodie's pick. Public only. Hidden during the show. Not a 366-title dump.

**SOUND stays OFF.** Four analog verbs if someone turns it on (threshold / acquire / cut / tape). No house WAVs on disk; procedural fallback. Official picture stays PLAY · MUTED. No licensed music. No voice.

The **show** (`/?present=1`) is that argument in ten unlabeled beats — not a slide deck. Beat 4 is the PUBLIC leftover (`/clip/channel-zero-never-aired`). Beat 5 is the combo (**1**). Beat 7 is official air: CH 07 ident, then the programmed network, PLAY · MUTED. Beat 8 is house Studio (CH 03). SOUND off. DISCOVER hidden. Constellation is a seventh surface (void / phone pocket), not in the show, not a seventh website.

A person, a place, a project is a **file of bonds**, not a profile. The rest of the node lives in the index.

The visitor should feel there is vastly more here than any documentary could contain — through mute remainder and year books, **not** a printed hour-count. They should be able to follow a person, a place, a tape, a year, a song, or a collection into a rabbit hole. They should never feel they are browsing a startup template, a streaming clone, or a portfolio.

## Honesty (this is still a prototype)

This answer is how the house is **already shaped**. It is not a claim that the filmmaker quality bar is met.

- Archive-only pictures are **PROTOTYPE MEDIA**. Official CH 07 / clip embeds are public YouTube, still not the vault tapes.
- Tape zoom is a **CSS camera** through the aisle, not an optical / R3F camera.
- World land is **authored rings**, not surveyed GIS.
- Real CC Television **show names are unknown**. Playlist names already on the channel may be used. Do not invent more.
- **~3,000 hours is not printed fact.** Neither is 330, 268, or any other working figure. Official listing is **366** unique public IDs; videos-tab badge **367**. Unlisted / private still missing.
- *Who Is Ernie Barnes?* stays **docs only**. Ghetto Rock / Culo / Tittyhead Ted / M.I.S. stay out of the product until James says otherwise.
- James still gates footage, official hour-count, Channel Zero year, shot-log, and real show names. Do not invent those answers.

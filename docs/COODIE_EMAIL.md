# Email draft — Coodie

**Subject:** I started building an idea for the Creative Control archive

Hey Coodie,

Sorry I didn’t get back to you sooner. The opportunity to help support your work on a project this important honestly sent me down a rabbit hole—in the best way.

I spent a good part of today studying the Creative Control history, Channel Zero, the current sites and the archive you’ve been teasing. I got pulled into the idea and started building instead of sending you another long explanation.

Here’s the early proof of concept:

https://creativecontrol-tv.vercel.app/?present=1

It’s best viewed on desktop. Click **NEXT** or press Space to walk through it. Sound is off by default.

This is intentionally an early concept, not me pretending to know the archive better than you do. I wanted to show how the same body of work might be experienced through several connected perspectives: as a world of places, a searchable historical index, a television network, a physical tape vault and a timeline spanning the work.

The more I looked at it, the more it felt like the opportunity is bigger than a conventional documentary or production-company website. Creative Control has always been a way of documenting culture while it is happening. The archive could become a place people explore, return to and discover relationships that no linear film could hold all at once.

I’ve attached a few stills so you can see the range, but the link is the real experience. Everything is still flexible, and the strongest next version would come from your language, real tape labels, confirmed dates and whatever material you want people to encounter first.

If it resonates, I’d love to walk you through it and hear what feels true, what doesn’t and where your mind is already going with the archive.

James

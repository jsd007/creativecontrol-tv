# Creative Control concept update, October 1

## Ready to demonstrate

- TV opens with six public starting points instead of the whole upload catalog. Search and program blocks reach the deeper listing, eight titles at a time.
- Previous and next follow the current selection. Reload, browser Back, and copied links preserve the title and guide context.
- On a phone, choosing a program or channel reveals the screen and focuses its title. Paging reveals the new guide page and focuses its heading.
- Clip details now include a return to the same TV selection, including its search, block, and page. This is a return path, not playback-progress restoration.
- The globe, index, source-tape explorer, and timeline retain their simpler entry points from the earlier refinement passes. The entrance stays unchanged.

## What to finish next

2. Reduce the timeline's deep year/month density. Keep six useful starting moments, add consistent example labels in deeper views, and favor one clear chapter over many controls.
3. Replace tape labels, dates, rights statuses, and stand-in frames with approved source material. The source explorer stays the default; the old aisle camera is not a prerequisite.
4. Give sparse TV house channels distinct, approved content. Studio currently has one title and Performances three; do not invent more footage to inflate them. Keep Broadcast as the strongest demo.
5. Replace stylized globe geography with better-authored coast geometry and test touch, keyboard, reduced motion, and WebGL fallback. Preserve the globe rather than returning to a flat-map default.
6. Review inferred people/place metadata with the team before treating it as archive truth. Public uploads are indexed examples, not proof of the private catalog's scope or rights.
7. After a small approved sample exists, plan ingest, transcripts, rights review, storage, and preservation. Do not add fake schedules, playback progress, memberships, or access controls to this proof.
